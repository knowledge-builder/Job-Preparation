# Finance Data Analytics & Product Intelligence Preparation

## Overview

This repository contains structured study notes, analytical exercises, and implementation projects focused on finance-oriented data analytics, product intelligence, operational metrics, and scalable reporting systems.

The goal is to strengthen practical skills in:

- Financial data analytics
- Product and operational metrics
- SQL and Python analytics workflows
- Statistical reasoning
- Dashboard development
- Aggregated data modeling
- Root-cause analysis
- Decision-support analytics

This preparation is aligned with modern fintech and product-driven analytics environments where data teams support product strategy, customer experience, and operational decision-making.
