### RECOMMENDED PRACTICE APPROACH
For every exercise:

1. **Explain the Operational Goal**
Example: “This workflow validates reporting completeness before dashboard publication.”

2. **Explain Risks**
Example: “Duplicate joins could inflate executive KPI metrics.”

3. **Explain Assumptions**
Example: “Incomplete workflows are excluded from SLA calculations.”

4. **Validate Results**
Always verify:
* row counts
* duplicate inflation
* null propagation
* aggregation correctness
* reconciliation consistency
* workflow-state validity
